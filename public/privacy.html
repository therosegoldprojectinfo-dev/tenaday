Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who We Are

Numio (numiomath.app) is an educational app for children aged 6–12. Parents create a family account; children use the app to study by photographing school materials and completing AI-generated quizzes.

2. What Data We Collect

  • Phone number (used as account identifier — never shared)
  • Children's first names (entered by parent)
  • Images of educational materials (processed by AI, not stored permanently)
  • Quiz results and coin balances
  • App usage data (quizzes generated, anonymous analytics)

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide the Numio service: generating educational quizzes, tracking learning progress, and managing the family reward system. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & PDPL)

Numio is designed for use by children under parental supervision. We do not collect personal data from children beyond their first name. All account management is handled by the parent. We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU GDPR for children, and the Saudi Arabia Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) issued by the National Data Management Office (NDMO).

5. AI Image Processing

Images you upload are sent to Anthropic's Claude API solely to generate quiz questions. Images are not stored by Numio after the quiz is generated. Please do not photograph personal documents, faces, or sensitive information.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure (servers located in the United States) with row-level security. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data transfers. Access to data is restricted to authenticated users only.

5a. Analytics & Advertising (Meta Pixel)

Numio uses the Meta Pixel (Facebook) to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns. Here is exactly what the pixel does and does not do:

  • App-wide base tracking: The Meta Pixel script loads every time the app is opened by anyone — parent or child — and sends a PageView event to Meta. This event contains only: the app URL (numiomath.app), a timestamp, device type, and IP address. It contains no name, no quiz data, no coins, and no information about who is using the app.
  • Onboarding conversion events: Additional named events (ViewContent, Lead, InitiateCheckout, CompleteRegistration) fire only during the parent account creation flow — specifically on the welcome, language selection, and phone/PIN screens. These events stop permanently once the account is created.
  • What is never sent to Meta: Child names, quiz questions or answers, coin balances, reward activity, or any in-app behaviour after signup.

Data collected by the Meta Pixel is processed by Meta Platforms, Inc. under their data policy. Parents can opt out at any time via Meta's privacy settings.

5b. Cross-Border Data Transfers (PDPL — Saudi Arabia)

In accordance with the Saudi Arabia Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), we disclose that your personal data is processed and stored outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia:

  • Supabase (United States) — stores account data, children's names, quiz results, and coin balances. Supabase is SOC 2 Type II certified and applies industry-standard security controls.
  • Anthropic (United States) — receives images you photograph solely to generate quiz questions. Images are not retained by Anthropic beyond the duration of the API request.
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (United States) — receives anonymised analytics data (page URL, timestamp, device type, IP address) via the Meta Pixel during parent onboarding. No child names, quiz data, or post-signup activity is shared. Meta is GDPR-certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

By creating a Numio account, you acknowledge and consent to this cross-border transfer of personal data as required under PDPL Article 29. We take all reasonable contractual and technical measures to ensure your data receives a level of protection equivalent to that required under Saudi law.

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request full deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

8. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
