About Numio

Numio is an educational technology product designed to make everyday school practice easier for children and parents.

Instead of requiring parents to search for exercises or create practice material themselves, they can photograph the lesson their child is currently studying and Numio creates personalized practice from that material.

Numio is designed to complement a child's existing education. It does not replace teachers, schools, or classroom instruction. Numio can be used across school subjects, allowing children aged 5–11 to practice different types of lessons in one place.
